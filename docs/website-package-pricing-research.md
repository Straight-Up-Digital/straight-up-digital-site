# Website package pricing: South African market checks

**Checked:** 25 November 2026. **Purpose:** support Straight Up Digital's proposed entry packages for Eastern Cape small businesses. Public listings are examples, not a like-for-like market average. Prices can change and some providers do not state whether VAT is included.

## Comparable published offers

**Five-page business website.** New Perspective Design in East London lists a five-page WordPress build at R5,590 once-off, excluding VAT, with responsive design, contact form, WhatsApp, a gallery, basic on-page SEO, two revision rounds and launch/handover. Advertising Solutions lists a five-page package at R2,950 once-off with domain and first-year hosting; Web Design Hosting lists R2,699 for five pages and says the primary package includes first-year hosting/domain. These lower offers do not document identical scope and support, so the lower prices should not be compared as equivalent to the East London package. [1] [2] [3]

**Restaurant or café.** Allsorts Web Designers in East London advertises general WordPress launches from R5,500 once-off. Forge Vertical in Gqeberha lists a general business site from R3,500, including a .co.za domain and first-year hosting; its restaurant example is illustrative, while bookings and online shops are quoted separately. The closest restaurant-specific fixed offer reviewed was R7,100 for a seven-page menu, hours, gallery, booking/contact and WhatsApp site. Telos offers a subscription alternative at R399/month for one page or R599/month for up to six pages, rather than a once-off build. [4] [5] [6] [7]

**Guest house or small lodge.** Creative Website Design lists a guest-house Starter at R2,950 once-off and a Business tier at R4,950 for up to seven pages. The starter uses links to third-party booking services and does not include two-way availability synchronisation. New Perspective Studio describes accommodation sites from around R5,000 for a linked-booking starter to R16,000–R18,000 for a custom booking system. StayHere offers a separate subscription model at R299/month. [8] [9] [10]

**Appointment and booking site.** Skapandi lists a three-page WordPress site at R5,000 plus a R3,000 booking add-on; payment setup is separately listed at R2,500. VB Dynamics lists a five-page site from R3,500 and a scheduling add-on from R1,200, without detailing calendar sync or payment. HOSTAFRICA's DIY website-and-scheduler offer is subscription-priced, so it is not equivalent to a custom agency build. [11] [12] [13]

**Small online shop.** iNetAxis lists a basic e-commerce package from R4,500 but does not specify a checkout/payment and inventory scope equivalent to a tested store. Pixaloom estimates R15,000–R25,000 for a focused store with catalogue, checkout and one hosted payment integration. Amandla lists broader WooCommerce builds at R20,000–R80,000+. WooCommerce's core software has no platform subscription or revenue share, but hosting and optional extensions cost extra. [14] [15] [16] [17]

The local references do not establish an Eastern Cape discount or a regional market average. They support a value-led local offer only when Straight Up Digital clearly limits its defined entry scopes. Hosting/domain renewals, licences, messaging, payment transaction costs, courier services, custom booking/channel-manager integrations and ongoing maintenance must be identified separately. For example, NightsBridge lists accommodation booking/channel-management plans from R660/month excluding VAT for 1–5 units; BookingPress lists a premium plugin licence from US$89/year; PayFast publishes transaction fees separately from website-build fees. [18] [19] [20]

The market comparisons above remain contextual examples only; they do not determine or amend SinJin's confirmed rates.

## Important limits before publication or sale

These owner-confirmed prices are **not independently verified** against Straight Up Digital's internal labour capacity, reseller costs, salesperson commissions, overhead, subscriptions, tax status or profit buffer. This note does not prove that every package covers company costs or commission. Compare delivery time and paid third-party costs with the applicable commission agreement and expense budget before making additional margin claims.

Every quote should say whether VAT applies, who supplies the copy and images, what the client owns, how many revision rounds are included, what hosting/domain renewals cost after any introductory term and what is excluded. Standalone website builds use 50% to start and 50% at launch after a preview; the discounted annual bundle is paid once-off in full. Do not describe a linked booking site as live availability on the new site. Do not promise live Paystack checkout for the shop until end-to-end testing passes and SinJin confirms.
