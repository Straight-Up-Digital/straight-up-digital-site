# Image provenance and use

Six visuals were AI-generated for this website as illustrative concepts, not client work. They were art-directed for the Straight Up Digital redesign. The five category concepts are optimized WebP files. The shared Africa network hero is also exported as WebP and is used behind the homepage visual and inner-page heroes. None of these visuals are customer photography, commissioned shoots, or evidence of business results.
