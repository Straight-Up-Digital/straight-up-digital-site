# Website redesign asset sourcing notes

Collected 27 November 2026 for the Straight Up Digital redesign. No new stock images have been downloaded or added as part of this redesign so far. The redesign reuses the existing three locally served Unsplash images, for which the repository already records individual image pages, photographers, file names, format conversion, and licence review in `docs/image-credits.md`.

The official Unsplash licence says its images may be downloaded and used free for commercial and non-commercial purposes, without permission or required attribution. It does not permit selling unmodified images or compiling them to replicate a competing stock service. Attribution is appreciated. Source: [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).

The official Pexels licence says its photos and videos are free to use and modify; attribution is not required. It prohibits, among other things, implying endorsement by people or brands shown and redistributing the images on stock-photo platforms. Source: [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

The image-search results surfaced eight direct image URLs without individual creator, title, source-page, or licence metadata. They are not being used because their provenance could not be confirmed from the results.

Existing credited photographs remain locally served in WebP format. Their captions describe business categories and do not claim to depict Straight Up Digital clients or completed projects. No image purchase or premium asset is involved.
